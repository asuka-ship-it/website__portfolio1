// 05 / Production mindset

The workflow is only useful if it survives imperfect data.

These are the controls I use to evaluate a GTM system before handoff. They make the difference between a demo that runs once and an operation a team can trust.

01

Validate inputs

Normalize required fields and catch missing or duplicate records before spending enrichment credits.

02

Route failures

Call the next provider only after a miss, reject invalid emails, and preserve an explicit exhausted state.

03

Gate AI output

Generate structured fields, check for missing context, and keep weak personalization out of activation.

04

Control handoff

Map clean fields into the CRM first, then launch only records that meet data and sending requirements.

Production test

I ask what happens when a provider returns nothing, an API times out, a record already exists, or an AI field is low-confidence. Those paths get designed before scale, not discovered during a live campaign. The find-and-verify waterfall built on these controls ran six layers across 46 contacts and returned a single verified email column.

CASE STUDY / 02 · HIRING-SIGNAL LEAD SCRAPER

GTM Lead Scraper

A company actively hiring for a GTM or RevOps role is a live buying signal, the same logic sales teams use to spot warm accounts. This pipeline scrapes live job postings for a target role, scores every result by fit, verifies who to actually contact, and drafts personalized outreach.

7Pipeline stages
4Real bugs found & fixed
$0.58Total cost, first full run

How I built it

  • Scraped live job postings for a target role using Apify Actors.
  • Scored every result against an auditable rubric before spending a single credit.
  • Enriched only the companies that were missing a contact.
  • Tested the enrichment Actor on 18 of them: it returned 6 “contacts,” but manual review found only 2 were real people.
  • Found the tool had flagged every guessed email as verified, including obvious fakes. Its check only proved the domain had a working mail server, not that the person’s inbox existed.
  • Wrote a deterministic filter that rejects fake contacts: job-board links, generic role labels, name-equals-company.
  • Ran a second, independent email verifier, catching a 67% false-positive rate the enrichment tool’s own “verified” label had missed.
  • Packaged the result as a reusable Claude Code skill and a public GitHub repo, with known limitations documented rather than glossed over.

Tools involved

  • Apify ActorsScraping live job postings from the target boards.
  • Custom PythonThe scoring rubric and the contact/email verification logic.
  • Claude CodePackaged the finished pipeline as a reusable skill.
  • GitHubPublished as a public repo with its limitations written down.

CASE STUDY / 03 · MULTI-SIGNAL SCORING + AI ARK ENRICHMENT

GTM Signal Stack

Most outbound lists start with a company name and a guess. This one starts with a live signal: companies actively hiring for GTM Engineer or RevOps roles, sourced straight from Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, not LinkedIn scraping.

50Companies scored, zero cost
5AI Ark-enriched, real firmographics
7Fully personalized, verified contacts

How I built it

  • Sourced companies hiring for GTM Engineer or RevOps roles straight from Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job boards.
  • Scored every company for free with a formula that reads its own job description for stack mentions like Clay, Salesforce and HubSpot.
  • Spent no enrichment credit at all until after the scoring pass had ranked the list.
  • Moved only the top scorers into AI Ark for firmographics and growth signals.
  • Generated personalized first liners, subject lines and full email bodies from the real signal data, not a template.
  • Hit a constraint: AI Ark’s structured API needed Clay’s paid HTTP column. Rather than upgrade, searched AI Ark’s own UI for the shortlist, exported the results and merged them back into Clay by hand. This smaller manual step that kept the whole pipeline on free tools without losing the data.

Tools involved

  • ClayThe scoring formula, the people lookup and the personalization columns.
  • AI ArkFirmographics and growth signals for the shortlisted companies only.
  • Greenhouse / Lever / AshbyThe hiring signal itself, straight from the source.

What it produced

50 companies scored for free by a formula reading each job posting for stack mentions, before any paid enrichment runs
AI Ark’s firmographics and growth signals written straight into the same Clay table as the job-signal data
Every enriched company exported from AI Ark as a clean, traceable CSV record
Contacts resolved per company, then carried into work email lookup and verification
Verified work emails alongside AI-generated first liners, subject lines and full email bodies, each referencing the real signal pulled earlier

CASE STUDY / 04 · WATERFALL ENRICHMENT

Caffinity: 6-layer email waterfall

Instead of stopping at “no email found,” this table cascades every contact through six alternating find + verify layers, trying a new data provider each time the previous one misses, until a valid work email is confirmed or every option is exhausted.

46Contacts in table
6Find + verify layers
29Data columns

How I built it

  • Cascade every contact through six alternating find + verify layers.
  • Try a new data provider each time the previous one misses.
  • Advance only when the previous result is actually missing or invalid, never on a whim.
  • Verify before accepting any address, so a found email is not assumed to be a real one.
  • Stop when a valid work email is confirmed, or when every option has been exhausted and the state is recorded explicitly.
  • Compile one verified address with a final formula, ready for CRM and campaign handoff.

Tools involved

  • ClayThe waterfall table, the conditional routing between layers and the final formula.
  • Multiple email providersEach one a separate find or verify layer in the cascade.

What it produced

Layers 1 to 3: Find work email → Verify email, repeated across three providers
Layers 4 to 6: continues the cascade, then compiles the final verified email via formula
